Add Camp Board to your Home Screen to continue

This board is built to run like a real app on your phone, so it needs to be installed first. It only takes a moment.

  1. 1Open this page in Safari (if you tapped this link inside another app, tap ••• or Share at the bottom, then "Open in Safari" first).
  2. 2Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), in Safari's toolbar.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4Tap Add in the top right.
  5. 5Close this browser tab, then open Camp Board from the icon that's now on your Home Screen.

Already added it? Go to your Home Screen and open the Camp Board icon from there — reloading this browser tab won't switch it over.
